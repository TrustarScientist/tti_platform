Meet the Founder

Built by someone who
lives the problem daily.

I've spent years teaching technology in Nigerian classrooms — and every day I saw firsthand what Nigerian education is not giving students. Smart, motivated young people who've been trained to pass exams but never shown how to build anything with what they know.

I built Trustar to close that gap. Not with more videos to watch — but with real projects to ship, real mentors who build things in the real world, and a platform that treats every student as a future creator, not just a future employee.

SchoolOS came out of the same conviction: schools deserve the same digital infrastructure that makes businesses run well. I'm building both simultaneously — one student, one school at a time.
