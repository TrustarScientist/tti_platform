Why Trustar Exists

Students aren't failing.
The system around them is.

Outdated curricula. Zero practical training. No mentorship pipeline. Certificates that employers quietly distrust. The result — everywhere we've looked, from Lagos to London — is the same: talented young people who studied hard and still can't build, create, or compete.

Trustar was built to close that gap — not with more videos to watch, but with real projects to build, real mentors to learn from, and real credentials tied to demonstrated capability.
